            Open web TV
          

          

            
          

          
The web TV page plays your channels in the browser. The menu link appears for everyone once there are channels; reload Jellyfin's web app after changing this.

          
          

          

            
Live TV stream

          

            
            
          

          

            
            
          

          

            
            
          

          
Burned-in subtitles show in every Live TV client and the web TV page. Programmes with a matching subtitle track are transcoded, which costs CPU. Channels can override this. The Cable TV app on your TV shows subtitles itself and doesn't need it.


          

            
Content groups

          
Named sets of sources that several channels can share: add a Content group source to a channel to include one. Changing a group changes every channel that uses it.

          


          

            
Commercials

          
Used by channels with commercials on that don't list their own. A Home videos library of ads works well.

          


          

          


          

            
Import and export

        
Import a lineup CSV (Channel Number,Channel Name,Title,Release Year, one row per show or movie), an episodes CSV (Show Title,Episode Title) for holiday specials, or a channel pack exported from this page. Titles are matched to your library by name; a year tells remakes apart. Check first to see what matches.
