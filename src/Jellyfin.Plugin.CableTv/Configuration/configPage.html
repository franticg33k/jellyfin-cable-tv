Cable TV

Channels built from your library, on a schedule that is the same on every device. They appear under Live TV once saved; how many days of listings apps show is set in Jellyfin's own Live TV settings.

Content groups

Named sets of sources that several channels can share: add a Content group source to a channel to include one. Changing a group changes every channel that uses it.
