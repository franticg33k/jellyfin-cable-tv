Cable TV

Channels built from your library, on a schedule that is the same on every device. They appear under Live TV once saved; how many days of listings clients show is set in Jellyfin's own Live TV settings.

Every schedule is computed from this instant. Changing it reshuffles all channels.

Live TV stream

How Live TV apps receive channels. Audio is always converted to AAC stereo.
Frame height when the stream transcodes, for example 720 or 1080.
Evens out volume between programmes and commercials.

Commercials

Used by channels with CommercialsEnabled that don't set their own CommercialSources. For example [{"Type": "Library", "Ids": ["<library id>"], "Values": []}].

Channels



          

            
            
            

              A list of channels. Source Type is Library, Collection, Playlist, Items (use Ids),
              Genre or Decade (use Values, e.g. ["Comedy"] or ["1990"]).
              Sorting is Random, Cyclic, RoundRobin, Block (with BlockSize) or Marathon;
              a source's Weight (1–10) makes its items air more often under Random.
              Breaks: CommercialsEnabled, GridMinutes (pad each programme to e.g. 30), MidBreak
              (None, Halfway, Chapter) and BreakSeconds (break length when there's no grid).
